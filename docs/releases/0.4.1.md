## Overlap 0.4.1

### New
- **Browse connected services in the main window.** The separate browser window is gone. Turn a service on from the small **Connected Services** menu at the bottom of the sidebar, and its section (Feed, Following, My Channels, search, your synced channels, Open Link…) appears below your tags. Results use the normal grid: thumbnail sizes, selection, **Space for Quick Look**, and the preview pane.
- **Drag to save.** Drag remote images onto a tag to save them to your library with that tag, or onto a mood board. Typing a tag in the quick-tag bar or pressing ⌘S saves too. Saved items show a check.
- **Search Are.na channels on any account.** Free accounts get *Search Channels*; Premium keeps full search.
- **Videos.** Video blocks play in the preview pane. Double-click or "Open Video" opens the video page in your browser. Saved videos keep their picture, with the link in the file's *Where from*.
- **Tag colors.** Finder's colors show in the sidebar and on tag chips. Set one from a tag's right-click menu; every file with that tag gets it, so Finder matches.
- New plugin setting for Are.na: *Also tag saved blocks with their channel* (off by default).

### Fixed
- Editing tags in Overlap no longer erases the colors Finder set on them.
- The Are.na folder no longer shows up in the queue.
- The queue's Group menu and "Suggestion sources" list show only suggestion plugins.
- Plugin replies with fractional-second dates no longer fail. Plugin messages and errors are now written to the system log (filter `[plugins]` in Console).
