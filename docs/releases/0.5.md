## Overlap 0.5

### New
- **Explore.** Press the **✦ Explore** button at the start of the results bar (⌘3) to see your library in batches of 24, steered by what you like. **Q** likes an image, **W** asks for less like it, **Space** steps to the next image, **E** shows the next batch and **R** opens Quick Look. You can change any of these keys in Settings. Your likes build up a set of *tastes* in the sidebar: click one to draw batches only from it, or right-click to forget it. Tags in the sidebar narrow what Explore draws from. **All Tags** goes back to the whole library. Liked images collect under **Liked**, and any batch can become a mood board. Explore needs the Overlap CLIP plugin. Your taste profile stays on this Mac.
- **Familiar ↔ Surprising.** A slider in the Explore bar sets how far batches stray from your tastes. Toward Surprising you get more random picks, more variety between images, and an even share for every taste. Right-click the slider to reset it.
- **Carousel preview.** The preview button now steps through off, beside the grid, and a carousel above the grid. Click and hold it to pick one directly.
- **Library and Queue** are named in the middle of the toolbar (⌘1 / ⌘2), and Queue shows how many items are waiting. To show icons instead, turn off *Show Library and Queue as words* in Settings.
- **Settings window** with a section for the toolbar and one for Explore keys.

### Changed
- **Find is now Query.** The Venn diagram view is part of the sidebar: switch between **Tags** and **Query** at the top of the sidebar (⌘D). Overlap remembers which one you used last.
- The tutorial follows the new layout.

### Plugins
- New `explore` capability: Overlap sends taste vectors and the plugin returns candidates. See docs/PLUGINS.md. Overlap CLIP supports it.
